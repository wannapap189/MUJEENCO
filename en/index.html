THE TEACHER

Ajarn Khaek.
Teachings and ritual practice.

Ajarn Khaek Muntra Siam conducts rituals and consecration for MUJEEN figures according to Maoshan Taoist feng shui teachings. After devotional materials are placed inside, he personally inscribes talismans and sacred characters on each figure once more.
