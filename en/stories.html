PERSONAL INSCRIPTIONS

By the teacher’s hand.
An inscription for each figure.

After devotional materials are placed inside, Ajarn Khaek Muntra Siam personally adds talismans and sacred characters to each figure once more, with attention to every detail.

Here, “inscribing” means adding talismans and sacred characters again after devotional materials have been placed inside. The teacher works on each figure individually before it is prepared for its future devotee.

DOOR GUARDIANS

Men Shen
A pair of door guardians.

In Chinese tradition, door guardians represent protection for the home and auspiciousness. Displayed as a pair at an entrance, they offer another expression of devotion in a personal space.

Men Shen form a separate devotional pairing, with their own meaning alongside the three-deity collection.
