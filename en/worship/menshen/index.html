門神 · DOOR GUARDIANS

Men Shen
A pair of door guardians.

A symbol of protection for the home and auspiciousness in Chinese tradition.

Men Shen are door guardians, traditionally displayed as a pair at the main entrance. They form a distinct devotional pairing for a home or personal place of worship.

THE MEANING

The meaning of the door guardians

Within the devotional tradition, Men Shen guard the threshold, ward off inauspicious influences and symbolise peace and stability for those within.

Consider the figures on the front together with the inscriptions on the reverse. Explore both in the gallery below.

PLACEMENT

Placement guidance

At the main entrance
At the front door of a home or building, according to the space.

As a pair, left and right
Keep both figures fully visible and aligned with the doorway.

Check the specific pair
The position and facing direction should follow the design of the particular pair and guidance from Muntra Siam.
